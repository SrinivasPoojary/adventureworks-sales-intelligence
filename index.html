Executive Insights & Recommendations

1. Category Margin Asymmetry

Bikes drive ₹ 28.32M (96.4%) of top-line revenue at 41% margin, while Accessories deliver 63% margin across 36,092 units. Automated cross-selling at checkout represents an immediate profit expansion vector.

2. Apparel Fulfillment Drag

Clothing generates only ₹ 1.36 Lakh in gross profit despite 9,101 units handled. Low-margin individual apparel items should be consolidated into curated cycling starter kits to reduce packing and shipping overhead.
